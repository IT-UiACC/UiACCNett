Our Mission

Our Story & Purpose

The Agder Case Hub

Founded by ambitious students at Campus Kristiansand, UiA Case Club was established to bring the international case solving tradition to the University of Agder. While university courses teach theory, real-world business requires synthesizing messy data under pressure, building convincing arguments, and presenting with boardroom presence.

Our Mission & Vision

We operate as an open, non-profit academic student society. We prepare our members for prestigious case competitions across Norway and the Nordics, connect top talent with industry leaders, and build a lasting culture of excellence in Kristiansand.

Methodology

The 4-Stage Solving Framework

Every session at UiA Case Club is designed around standard frameworks used by leading management consulting and strategy advisory firms worldwide.

01

1. Diagnose Bottlenecks

Deconstruct ambiguous corporate dilemmas using MECE and Issue Trees to identify root causes instead of superficial symptoms.

02

2. Quantify & Model

Run rapid sanity estimations, unit economics, and financial feasibility models to ground every strategy in sound data.

03

3. Executive Storytelling

Craft clean, concise slide decks following pyramid logic to make complex strategic roadmaps instantly understandable.

04

4. The Jury Pitch

Pitch recommendations live to C-level executives and corporate juries, followed by rigorous questioning and debate.

Who Can Join?

All registered students at the University of Agder are warmly welcomed. Whether you are studying Business, Economics, IT, Software Engineering, Law, Political Science, or Humanities, your unique background brings vital perspectives to a case team. No prior experience is required.
