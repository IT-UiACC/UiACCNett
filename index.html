Deep Dive

What is a Case Club?

An international university tradition bridging academic theory and executive strategic leadership.

The Case Concept

A Case Club is a student-driven organization focused on solving real-world business challenges using the case method pioneered by top global institutions. Students step into the shoes of CEOs, board members, or strategy consultants to diagnose complex organizational bottlenecks, develop data-backed solutions, and pitch their recommendations directly to industry leaders.

What We Do in Practice

We run hands-on workshops, mock competitions, and company case nights. Members work in teams under timed conditions to analyze messy data, structure arguments using frameworks like MECE and Issue Trees, build clean executive slide decks, and deliver compelling presentations followed by rigorous Q&A sessions.

Dual Benefits: Learning & Social

Learning & Career Growth

Develop practical strategic thinking, quantitative modeling, slide craft, and high-pressure communication. Case solving is one of the single most recognized student activities by top consulting firms, investment banks, and multinational companies.

Community & Network

Connect with ambitious peers across IT, Business, Engineering, Law, and Social Sciences. Enjoy collaborative pizza sessions, social gatherings, and the thrill of representing UiA at national competitions.

UiA Case Club In-Depth

UiA Case Club is the student-driven case solving hub at the University of Agder. We are 100% open to all students across every study discipline and study year, with no prior experience required. Our mission is to empower UiA students with world-class problem-solving capabilities while representing Agder on the national stage.
